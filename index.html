Welcome to The Old Stable

The Old stable is a cosy guest house located right behind Chipping Sodbury High Street, where you'll find plenty of pubs, restaurants, and shops.
This small two-floor guest house has everything you need for your stay on the edge of the Cotswolds and has a separate entrance through the garden.

About our space

On the ground floor, you will find a kitchenette with the essentials for breakfast and a quick bite, a square dining table, and a sofa bed.
The bedroom (upstairs) has a double bed, wardrobe, and a small bathroom.
Please note that the shower is quite short. If you are over 6 foot, it might be a tight squeeze!
The stairs are also quite narrow and steep, so they are not suitable for people with mobility issues.

Meet the team

The Old Stable is entirely managed by us, Gaia and Adam. We live at the same address and have recently renovated and decorated the space.
We will try our best to meet you when you arrive, but we also have a lockbox so you can self check-in (and check-out) at your convenience.
During your stay, you might also meet our two purr-fect assistants, Freya and Nya!
