About Our Space

The Old Stable is a cozy guest house equipped with everything you need for a comfortable stay on the edge of the Cotswolds, featuring a separate private entrance through the garden.

Ground Floor: Kitchenette with essentials for breakfast and a quick bite, a square dining table, and a sofa bed.

Upper Floor: Bedroom with a double bed, wardrobe, and a compact bathroom.

ℹ️ Please Note: The shower is quite short (if you are over 6ft tall, it might be a tight squeeze!) and the stairs are narrow and steep, so the space is not suitable for guests with mobility issues.
