How To Find Us

The Old Stable • Guest House Directions

Drive to Melbourne Drive

Private access to the guest house is located at the back of the property on Melbourne Drive.

📍 56 Melbourne Drive, Chipping Sodbury, Bristol BS37 6LB

1
Find the Entrance Gate

Opposite no. 56, look for a small green gate with a sign for "17 Horse Street - rear entrance".

🔓 Gate Access: The gate is not locked! Just slide the latch handle to the right to open.
2
Follow the Path Through the Garden

The back entrance and garden path are shared between numbers 13, 15, and 17. Go straight along the gravel path and then curve around to the right.

3
Enter the Courtyard

Keep following the path. On your left, you will find a small courtyard. The guest house is situated on the left-hand side.

4
You Have Arrived!

Welcome to The Old Stable! You've reached your accommodation.
