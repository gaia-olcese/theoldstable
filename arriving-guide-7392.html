How To Find Us

The Old Stable • Guest House Directions

Drive to Melbourne Drive

Private access to the guest house is from the back of the property, on Melbourne Drive.

📍 56 Melbourne Drive, Chipping Sodbury, Bristol BS37 6LB
🚗 Parking Info: If you are driving, the best place to park is Melbourne Drive. Drive all the way towards the end, as there is normally plenty of on-street parking around there.
🗺️ Google Maps 🌐 Our Website
1
Find the Entrance Gate

Opposite no. 56, look for a small green gate with a sign for "17 Horse Street - rear entrance".

🔓 Gate Access: The gate is not locked! Just slide the latch handle to the right to open.
2
Follow the Garden Path (Straight On)

The back entrance and part of the garden are shared between numbers 13, 15, and 17. Head straight along the paved path alongside the wall.

3
Continue Path around to the Right

Keep following the garden path as it curves around to the right past the seating area.

4
Enter the Small Courtyard

On your left, you will find a small courtyard. The guest house entrance is on the left side!

5
You Have Arrived & Entering

Welcome to The Old Stable! You have reached your accommodation.

🔑 Door & Key Instructions: You can open the left door using the key.

🔒 To Lock: Push the door handle up before turning the key!
